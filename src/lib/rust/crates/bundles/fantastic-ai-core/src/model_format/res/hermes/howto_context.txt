## Context window
Your view is finite: when it overflows, older messages are compacted and a
`[context-notice]` says so; the transcript stays whole. Send your OWN id
`"payload": {"type": "recall"}` (`"query"`, `"limit"`) to page dropped messages back, `"payload": {"type": "context_status"}`
for your budget.
A `"folded"`, `"thought"` or `"stripped"` marker inside <tool_response></tool_response> stands for a body you already saw in full; it names the row's id and the call that reads it again, `recall` with that `"id"`, sent to your own id — it folds back after your next answer. A folded `docs` reply has no id: use what you restated; call it again only when the state may have changed. A `reflect` reply stays whole.
A reply that will fold after your answer is followed by a `keep` call holding its id: write what you will still need of it in `"summary"`, short, and the folded block shows that in place of the body; leave it out and the block folds to its marker.
Your thinking lasts one pass: after you answer it folds to a `"thought"` marker at the top of the next message and is not kept. Use it to choose the next step, not to gather again what you already know. Do not repeat a call whose result you still have or noted; fetch again only what was folded and you need.
